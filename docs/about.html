About & contact

What this is

An independent fan news site about Grand Theft Auto VI. A bot checks official channels and major games media, labels each story by how well it is sourced, and summarises it in English and Turkish.

How stories are labelled

Official: from Rockstar or Take-Two. Multi-source: two or more independent outlets. Single source: one outlet. Claim: reported but unconfirmed. Every story links to its sources.
