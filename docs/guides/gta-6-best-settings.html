GTA 6 best settings for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

Updated: 2026-11-17

Short answer

Rockstar has not published GTA 6's in-game graphics options yet; this page adds them at launch. Before you start, calibrate HDR in your console settings, enable 120 Hz and VRR if your TV supports them, and install the game on internal SSD storage.

Console settings to check now

  • Run the system HDR calibration (PS5: Settings › Screen and Video; Xbox: Settings › General › TV & display options).
  • Turn on 120 Hz output and VRR if your TV or monitor supports them.
  • Set your TV to Game Mode to cut input lag.
  • Install GTA 6 on the internal SSD or an NVMe expansion, not a USB drive.
