GTA 6 file size and pre-load: how much space you need

Updated: 2026-11-17

Short answer

Rockstar's FAQ lists GTA 6 at up to 212 GB on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital pre-orders could pre-load from November 10, 2026; boxed copies (download code) from November 12.

Advertisement

Pre-load times

  • PS5 digital: from midnight local time on November 10, 2026.
  • Xbox Series X|S digital: from 12 a.m. GMT on November 10, 2026.
  • Boxed edition: the box holds a download code, usable for pre-load from November 12.
  • The game stays locked until launch at midnight local time on November 19.
Advertisement

How much free space to keep

Plan for the full 212 GB plus room for a day-one patch. Rockstar has not announced the patch size, so keeping 230–250 GB free is the safe choice.

Ways to free up space

  • Move older games to an external USB drive (PS5 and Xbox can store last-gen titles there).
  • On PS5, add an M.2 NVMe SSD with a heatsink in the expansion slot.
  • On Xbox Series X|S, use an official storage expansion card.
  • Delete game captures and videos you no longer need.

FAQ

Is GTA 6 212 GB on every console?

Rockstar says up to 212 GB depending on the device; the exact size can differ slightly between PS5 and Xbox.

Is there a PC version?

Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC release date.
