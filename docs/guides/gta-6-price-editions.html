GTA 6 price: Standard vs Ultimate Edition, by country

Updated: 2026-11-17

Short answer

In the US, GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. The UK price is £69.99 / £89.99, and the Ultimate upgrade for Standard owners is $20 / £20.
