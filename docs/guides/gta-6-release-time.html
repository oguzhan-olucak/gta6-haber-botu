GTA 6 release time: when it unlocks in your region

Updated: 2026-11-17

Short answer

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, based on PlayStation Store listings. New Zealand goes first; the US unlocks at midnight Eastern (9 p.m. Pacific on November 18).

Unlock times by region

  • New Zealand: midnight NZDT (11:00 UTC, November 18) — first in the world.
  • Australia: midnight AEDT (13:00 UTC, November 18).
  • Turkey and Saudi Arabia: midnight UTC+3 (21:00 UTC, November 18).
  • Continental Europe: midnight CET (23:00 UTC, November 18).
  • UK and Ireland: midnight GMT (00:00 UTC, November 19).
  • USA and Canada: midnight Eastern (05:00 UTC, November 19); West Coast plays from 9 p.m. on November 18.

What decides your unlock time

Your account's store region, not your console clock. Changing the clock does not move the unlock.
